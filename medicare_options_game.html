Medicare Options Compared

A plain-English guide to Original Medicare, Medicare Advantage, and Medigap. It is for learning only, not advice.

Key rule: You pick one path: (A) Original Medicare, with or without a Medigap policy, or (B) Medicare Advantage. You cannot use Medigap with Medicare Advantage.
